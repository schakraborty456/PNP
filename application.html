SECTOR 01 • MOBILITY & SAFETY
CRITICAL LIFE-SAFETY APPLICATION

Automotive Restraints, Airbags & Interior Trims

Vehicle passenger safety demands uncompromising engineering precision. PNP supplies certified high-tenacity NYTEX Nylon 6 FDY (840D, 1260D, 1680D) engineered with a superior tensile tenacity ≥ 8.2 gpd and controlled elongation curves, specifically calibrated to cushion kinetic shock during crash deceleration.

Passenger Seat Belts: Tensile breaking load > 2,600 kg with high abrasion resistance over 50,000 buckle cycles.
Helmet Straps & Harnesses: Zero edge-fraying narrow woven webbings complying with international crash protection codes.
Airbag Technical Fabrics: High burst pressure resistance and pyrotechnic thermal stability during microsecond deployment.
Acoustic Interior Trims: Sound-dampening laminated textile backings for headliners and door panels.
Tenacity ≥ 8.2 gpd FMVSS 302 Flammability ISO 9001:2015 Audited Heat Stabilized Tight Tensile Tolerance ±2%
SECTOR 02 • ACTIVE PERFORMANCE
TECHNICAL TEXTILE ENGINEERING

High-Performance Sportswear, Swimwear & Protective Gear

Modern athletes require fabrics that seamlessly balance thermal regulation, 4-way anatomical stretch, and rugged abrasion endurance. NYTEX Air Covered Yarn (ACY) with Lycra/Spandex and DTY microfibers provide superior moisture transport, chlorine immunity, and compression recovery.

Athletic Compression Tights: High modulus recovery nylon blended with spandex for muscle vibration reduction.
UV & Saltwater Swimwear: Chlorine-resistant and UPF 50+ UV protection for competitive racing suits and beachwear.
Protective Sports Padding: Ballistic outer fabrics for cricket batting pads, hockey shinguards, and tactical knee braces.
Performance Athletic Socks: Dope-dyed antimicrobial filaments with high friction endurance across heel and toe zones.
NYTEX ACY Air-Covered 4-Way Anatomical Stretch UPF 50+ Barrier Zero Water Dope-Dyeing Rapid Perspiration Evaporation
SECTOR 03 • APPAREL & HOSIERY
TOUCH, DRAPABILITY & SKIN COMFORT

Fine Apparel, Intimate Wear, Stockings & Laces

In intimate wear and women's fashion, skin feel, drape, and snag resistance define consumer loyalty. NYTEX POY and DTY yarns (20D to 70D in Semi-Dull and Full-Dull) deliver a silk-like hand touch, uniform tension, and zero skin irritation certified under OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class 1.

Stockings & Pantyhose: Ultra-fine 20D/7F and 40D/12F yarns providing smooth ladder-resistant stretch and contour fit.
Lingerie & Intimate Wear: Silk-like microfilament counts delivering breathability and zero seam chafing.
Ethnic Sarees & Ornate Laces: Bright trilobal FDY yarns offering intense light reflection for shimmering borders.
Outerwear Windcheaters: Featherweight woven micro-ripstop fabrics with rain-repellent coatings.
OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class 1 Silk-Touch Microfiber Trilobal Bright Filaments Circular & Warp Knit Optimized
SECTOR 04 • INFRASTRUCTURE & PEB
STRUCTURAL HARDWARE & ZERO-POWER VENTILATION

Pre-Engineered Buildings, Roofing & Industrial Sheds

India's rapid industrialization depends on resilient pre-engineered steel buildings (PEBs). PNP delivers high-strength case-hardened self-drilling fasteners and wind-driven roof turbo ventilators to secure industrial roofs against tropical monsoons and purge toxic factory heat at zero electrical cost.

TIE Self-Drilling Screws: Case-hardened C1022 steel piercing up to 12.5mm hot-rolled structural steel in under 5 seconds.
HAO Roof Turbo Ventilators: Assembled in India, starting at 3 km/h wind velocity to exhaust hot air 24/7 with zero electric bills.
EPDM Bonded Sealing Washers: Vulcanized true EPDM seals eliminating roof leaks and electrolytic bimetallic corrosion.
Monsoon Industrial Tarpaulins: PVC-coated high-density fabrics protecting open grain yards, cement, and logistics rakes.
Ruspert 1000-Hr Salt Spray 0W Electrical Operation Assembled in India Drill Capacity ≤ 12.5mm Steel SS304 / Al Alloy Vanes
SECTOR 05 • LOGISTICS & INTRALOGISTICS
HEAVY-DUTY STORAGE INFRASTRUCTURE

Warehouse Pallet Racking, Freight Covers & Dunnage

Managing high-density storage and supply chain protection across India's e-commerce, third-party logistics (3PL), and manufacturing hubs. PNP engineers structural pallet racking systems rated for thousands of kilograms alongside heavy-duty transit containment fabrics.

PNP Pallet Racking Systems: High-tensile IS 2062 Grade steel beams and uprights supporting up to 4,000 kg per pallet level.
Container Liners & Webbings: High-tenacity 1680D nylon tie-downs and lashing straps for maritime and rail freight.
Waterproof Dunnage Tarpaulins: 2000mm+ water column hydrostatic barrier protecting open logistics wagons and sheds.
Electrostatic Powder Coating: Automated 7-tank pre-treatment and epoxy-polyester coating for scratch and rust resistance.
IS 2062 Structural Steel 4,000 kg/Level Beam Capacity Automated Epoxy Powder Coating Custom Bay Layouts
SECTOR 06 • GREEN ARCHITECTURE
SYNWOOD & HAOWOOD • 10% NATIONAL MARKET SHARE

Waterproof Eco-Timber Cladding, Louvers & Modular Interiors

Transforming modern architecture and luxury hospitality by replacing vulnerable natural timber with 100% recyclable, zero-wood polymer composite boards. Holding 10% market share in India with 21 downloadable technical catalogues, Synwood delivers timeless wood grain without cutting a single forest tree.

Exterior Facade Louvers: Weatherproof exterior architectural fins that never warp, swell, or peel under monsoon rain or UV radiation.
Modular Wet Area Carcasses: 100% waterproof synthetic wood for hotel bathroom vanities, modular kitchens, and pool decks.
Fluted Wall Panelling: Sleek acoustic wall cladding for corporate conference rooms, luxury residences, and retail boutiques.
Precision CNC Jali Screens: Clean edge routing and high-density screw holding capacity without edge chipping.
