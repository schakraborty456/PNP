CORPORATE FOUNDATION & HERITAGE

Built on Sincerity, Hard Work & Customer Loyalty

From introducing the world's first branded luggage fabric in 2002 to operating world-class German polymer texturizing and continuous polymerization lines.

PNP Polytex Private Limited was founded in 1997 on immutable ethical principles: uncompromising technical sincerity, relentless hard work, and steadfast loyalty to customers. These core pillars have propelled PNP into India's No. 1 brand for Coated Cloth and industrial technical fabrics, commanding over 25% of the national market share.

Under the direction of Founder & Managing Director Mr. Pawan Kumar Kaushik, PNP revolutionized the Indian luggage industry in 2002 by becoming the first company worldwide to introduce branded luggage fabrics with standardized technical parameters, guaranteed tensile strength, and consistent denier formulations across 100+ specialized varieties.

Today, PNP Group's vertically integrated operations encompass our modern Bhilad Polymerization Plant (Gujarat) manufacturing high-tenacity NYTEX Nylon 6 filament yarns on German Oerlikon Barmag machinery, HAO Wind-Powered Turbo Ventilators (assembled in India), TIE Hardened PEB Self-Drilling Fasteners, SYNWOOD 100% recyclable synthetic timber capturing 10% national market share, and BAGINNOV, India's largest 25,000 sq. ft. luggage experience center in Borivali (West), Mumbai.

CORPORATE EVOLUTION

Key Milestones in Our 29-Year Journey

A chronological look at how continuous innovation, domestic manufacturing, and client loyalty built an industrial leader.

1997

Inception of PNP Group

Founded in Mumbai by Mr. Pawan Kumar Kaushik with a mission to eliminate intermediary volatility and supply certified technical textiles with transparent, fair commercial terms.

2002

World's 1st Branded Luggage Fabric

PNP revolutionized the Indian luggage industry by introducing branded coated cloth with standardized denier specifications, tensile guarantees, and 100+ specialized color formulations.

2008

Bhilad Polymer Spinning Facility

Established PNP Polymers Pvt. Ltd. at Bhilad, Gujarat, commissioning multi-filament continuous polymerization spinning lines for high-tenacity NYTEX Nylon 6 yarn.

2014

Industrial Infrastructure: HAO & TIE

Diversified into pre-engineered building solutions with HAO Wind-Powered Turbo Ventilators (assembled in India) and TIE hardened self-drilling PEB roofing fasteners.

2018

German Barmag Tech & BAGINNOV

Installed latest German Oerlikon Barmag high-speed texturizing lines at Bhilad; inaugurated India's largest 25,000 sq. ft. BAGINNOV multi-brand luggage showroom in Mumbai.

2022 – 2026

National Honor & 100% Green Horizon

Conferred The Times of India Inspiring Entrepreneurs Award; achieved 100% captive wind/solar power at Bhilad plant and captured 10% national share with SYNWOOD eco-timber.
