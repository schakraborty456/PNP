SPINNING EXCELLENCE • SECOND LARGEST IN INDIA

NYTEX High-Tenacity Nylon 6 Filament Yarn

Established in 2018 at Bhilad, Gujarat, NYTEX is India's second largest nylon filament yarn manufacturer, equipped with the latest German Oerlikon Barmag machinery with WINGS winders and powered by 100% clean green energy.

#2 Largest in India 100% Wind & Solar Green Energy German Oerlikon Barmag Tech (WINGS) Bhilad Plant (Est. 2018) 10 Indian Offices + Taipei Global Office

NYTEX is PNP Group's flagship manufacturing brand of Nylon 6 filament yarn, trusted by India's foremost textile mills, circular knitters, warp knitters, and narrow fabric weavers. Produced under strict computerized temperature and tension controls at PNP Polymers Pvt. Ltd., Bhilad (Gujarat), NYTEX sets the benchmark for softness, tenacity, elongation balance, and abrasion resistance.

Our plant produces a comprehensive spectrum of 5 specialized yarn types ranging from 20D to 1680D in Bright, Semi-Dull, and Custom Dope-Dyed colors.

GERMAN PRECISION ENGINEERING • LAST BORN CHILD IN INDIA

State-of-the-Art Oerlikon Barmag Spinning Lines

As the latest major nylon spinning plant established in India (2018), NYTEX is equipped with the newest generation of German Oerlikon Barmag continuous extrusion lines and cutting-edge WINGS winders.

Technical Consultation

Barmag WINGS Winders

High-speed winding up to 6,000 m/min with computerized package build, uniform tension, and zero loop formation during high-speed unwinding.

Continuous Polymerization

Computerized melt manifold with sintered metal micro-filtration ensuring 100% defect-free filament extrusion with zero gel spots.

Heated Godet Drawing

Multi-stage induction-heated Godets provide flawless thermal crystallization, superior tenacity (≥ 5.2 to 8.2 gpd), and stable elongation.

Precision Intermingling

Ultrasonic micro-jet ceramic nozzles guarantee exact entanglement frequency per meter, eliminating weaving and knitting stoppages.

INFRASTRUCTURE & ADVANCED PROCESSES

Modern Machinery & Clean Production Lines

Our capital investments in German precision engineering ensure micron-level uniformity across every single spool of NYTEX yarn.

Micro-Filtration Extrusion Towers

Automated polymer melt pressure regulation with high-density sintered metal filter media for knot-free, gel-free filament extrusion.

High-Speed Texturizing (DTY)

Equipped with heated Godet rollers and ceramic friction discs for consistent bulk density, balanced torque, and dynamic stretch elasticity.

Oerlikon Barmag WINGS Winders

Next-gen take-up winders operating up to 6,000 m/min with computerized package build geometry and zero ribboning defects.

Ultrasonic Air Covering (ACY)

High-precision micro-jet air intermingling chambers intimately bonding Nylon 6 sheath filaments with elastane core fibres.

INNOVATION & TESTING

Polymer Testing Laboratory & Custom Formulations

Our in-house R&D team collaborates with leading textile institutions and industrial clients to formulate custom polymer blends and specialty deniers.

R&D Capabilities

  • Computerized Spectrophotometer color-matching for exact batch dye repeatability.
  • Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC) for polymer thermal transition analysis.
  • Tensile & Elongation testing up to 10,000 N under controlled standard atmospheric humidity.
  • Melt Flow Index (MFI) and intrinsic viscosity validation for raw polymer batches.

At PNP Polymers, continuous improvement is an institutional mandate. Whether formulating ultra-fine 20-denier monofilament for luxury hosiery or high-tenacity 1680D ballistic nylon for military and aerospace load carriers, our laboratory validates every structural characteristic before commercial production runs.

We also develop dope-dyed color formulations, integrating masterbatch pigments directly at the polymer melt phase to eliminate post-spinning water-intensive dye baths and ensure zero color bleeding.

ZERO DEFECT COMMITMENT

10-Point Rigorous Quality Control

Every batch of NYTEX yarn passes through ten uncompromising physical, chemical, and optical inspection gates.

Checkpoint 01 - 03

Denier, Tenacity & Elongation

Automatic denier skein testing, single-end break tensile evaluation, and percentage elongation validation against ISO 2062 standards.

Checkpoint 04 - 06

Draw Tension & Crimp Contraction

Uster tensor analysis, boiling water shrinkage (BWS) stability, and crimp contraction frequency verification for uniform stretch behavior.

Checkpoint 07 - 10

Color Fastness & Optical Inspection

Spectrophotometer color delta-E analysis, intermingle point frequency count, UV resistance, and 100% automated optical camera bobbin inspection.
