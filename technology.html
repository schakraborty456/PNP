High-Precision Machinery & Automation

Equipped with world-class spinning towers, texturizing machines, water-jet weaving looms, and knife-coating lines ensuring micron-level filament uniformity.

PRODUCTION EXCELLENCE

State-of-the-Art Plant Infrastructure

Our capital investments in precision German and Italian engineering eliminate batch variations and guarantee superior mechanical performance.

Extrusion Towers

Automated polymer melt temperature regulation with micro-pore sintered metal filter packs ensuring knot-free filament extrusion.

High-Speed Texturizing

Equipped with heated Godet rollers, ceramic friction discs, and computerized intermingling jets for bulk density and elasticity control.

Water-Jet Weaving

High-density water-jet weaving looms producing defect-free base fabrics for luggage, apparel, and technical tarpaulins up to 220 cm width.

Continuous Coating Lines

Multi-roller coating systems applying waterproof PVC, PU, and breathable coatings with 3-stage curing ovens and pinhole-free consistency.
