Deep-Rooted Environmental Commitments Across All Divisions

PILLAR 01 • RENEWABLE POWER
CARBON FOOTPRINT MITIGATION

100% Wind & Rooftop Solar Clean Energy at Bhilad

Operating high-speed German Oerlikon Barmag texturizing machinery and continuous 24/7 nylon extrusion requires substantial energy. PNP Polymers has decarbonized our primary manufacturing hub at Bhilad, Gujarat by integrating on-site rooftop solar photovoltaic arrays with dedicated captive wind turbine farms. By operating with clean, zero-emission electricity, we eliminate thousands of metric tons of Scope-2 CO₂ emissions every year, ensuring every yarn package carries a dramatically lower embodied carbon footprint for our downstream brand partners.

10,000+ MWh Clean green power generated annually via captive wind & solar assets, offsetting industrial grid coal consumption.
100% Renewable Extrusion Captive Wind Turbines Rooftop Solar PV Installation Scope-2 Carbon Abatement Bhilad Eco-Manufacturing Hub
PILLAR 02 • WATER STEWARDSHIP
ZERO CONTAMINATION & ZERO LIQUID DISCHARGE (ZLD)

Zero-Water Dope-Dyeing & 95%+ Closed-Loop Water Recycling

Traditional package wet-dyeing is among the most environmentally damaging industrial processes, consuming up to 100 litres of fresh water per kilogram of yarn while producing contaminated chemical effluent. PNP eliminates this ecological hazard through state-of-the-art dope-dyeing (spin-dyeing). Micro-ground color masterbatches and UV stabilizers are compounded directly into the molten nylon polymer before extrusion. The result is zero toxic dye baths, 100% molecular color penetration that resists bleaching and laundering, and zero dye wastewater release.

0 Litres Toxic chemical dye effluent generated. Plus >95% process water recycled via advanced closed-loop cooling and weaving cycles.
Zero-Water Spin Dyeing 0L Toxic Effluent Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) Permanent Colorfastness Closed-Loop Cooling Towers
PILLAR 03 • CIRCULAR ECONOMY
WASTE DIVERSION & RECLAIMED NYLON

Closed-Loop Polymer Reclamation & Scrap Upcycling

Guided by circular economy principles, PNP actively diverts non-biodegradable synthetic polymers from landfills. In-plant spinning transitions, fiber trimmings, and end-of-cone filaments are collected, mechanically depolymerized or compounded, and re-extruded into high-grade engineering polymers. Aligned with the Global Recycled Standard (GRS), our reclaimed nylon derivatives retain tensile tenacity and elasticity matching virgin polymers, enabling luggage and technical textile brands to fulfill strict Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) mandates.

100% Internal spinning transitions and edge trimmings collected and diverted into circular secondary compounding.
Global Recycled Standard (GRS) In-Plant Polymer Re-Pelletizing EPR Mandate Alignment Zero Landfill Waste Objective Circular Supply Chain
PILLAR 04 • NATURE CONSERVATION
BIODIVERSITY & HABITAT PRESERVATION

Preserving Natural Forests: SYNWOOD 100% Recyclable Eco-Timber

Deforestation across India depletes natural carbon sinks and accelerates ecological loss. PNP’s SYNWOOD and HAOWOOD divisions provide architects and commercial interior fitters with an advanced polymer composite board that replaces traditional plywood, MDF, and teak wood. SYNWOOD requires zero wood flour, emits zero toxic formaldehyde or carcinogenic VOCs, is completely immune to termites and water rot, and is 100% recyclable at the end of its life cycle into new structural panels.

10% Share National synthetic wood timber-alternative market captured, sparing thousands of mature forest trees each year.
Zero Trees Felled Formaldehyde-Free (Zero VOC) 100% Waterproof & Termite-Proof End-of-Life 100% Recyclable IGBC Green Building Credits
PILLAR 05 • PASSIVE COOLING
CLEAN INDUSTRIAL ATMOSPHERE

Zero-Electricity Industrial Cooling: HAO Natural Turbo Ventilators

Industrial manufacturing facilities and expansive PEB logistics parks typically spend massive electrical energy running heavy forced-air exhaust fans. PNP’s HAO Wind-Powered Turbo Ventilators (assembled in India) provide a 100% electricity-free convective cooling solution. Utilizing natural atmospheric thermal buoyancy (stack effect) combined with external wind currents starting at just 3 km/h, HAO ventilators continuously exhaust hot stagnant air, humid gases, and toxic fumes with zero carbon emissions.

0 Watts Continuous electricity drawn across a 15+ year operational lifespan with permanently sealed Japanese dual bearings.
Zero Operating Energy Cost Natural Stack Effect Convection Assembled in India Rust-Proof Stainless Steel Zero Operational Carbon
